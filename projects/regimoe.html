RegiMoE
Regime-Gated Downscaling

RegiMoE is a regime-gated mixture of experts: a gating network classifies each day's thermodynamic regime and routes it to regime-specialised experts, so similar environmental conditions share a predictor. It performs statistical downscaling and bias correction of coarse ERA5 reanalysis against ground-station observations, to make heat risk visible where displaced and under-monitored people live, in support of their rights to life, security and an adequate standard of living (UDHR Articles 3 and 25).

Overview

Coarse reanalysis smooths away the local heat that matters most to people living in camps, settlements and under-monitored regions. A single statistical correction applied everywhere also averages over very different weather: a humid coastal day, a dry desert afternoon and a stable mountain night carry different biases. RegiMoE learns those differences explicitly. A gate reads the day's thermodynamic state as anomalies from climatology and assigns it softly to a small set of regimes; each regime has its own expert that corrects the coarse field toward what ground stations record.

The work builds directly on the physics-informed SRGAN proposal for this fellowship, which introduced a Thermodynamic Regime Classifier and regional calibrators. Here the regime gate becomes the core of the model and is tested as rigorously as any baseline. Adversarial fine-tuning and gridded super-resolution are deferred to Spring 2027 so that this term delivers station-validated results first.

The project is developed in a California sandbox, where stations are dense and climates diverse, then transferred to the Horn of Africa, where stations are few and the humanitarian stakes are high. Every claim is judged on stations the model has never seen, in a year held out until the baselines are frozen.

Methodology

Regime-Gated Mixture of Experts

The gate sees only climatological anomalies of temperature, humidity, pressure, wind, radiation and cloud, plus one solar term. It never sees latitude, longitude, station identity or the calendar, and falsifiable tests check that it has not learned them indirectly. Experts share a trunk and specialise in regime-specific heads, trained with a load-balancing term so that no single expert takes over.

Downscaling and Evaluation

Models predict the residual between station observations and the elevation-corrected coarse value, then apply to the 0.1° grid for maps. Each result is compared with a frozen ladder of baselines, including quantile mapping, gradient-boosted trees and a single network matched in size and tuning effort, using leave-station-out and spatial-block cross-validation with bootstrap confidence intervals.

Data Description

Coarse Model Input

ERA5 Reanalysis (0.25°)

Hourly ECMWF reanalysis reduced on Google Earth Engine to daily maximum, minimum and mean temperature, humidity, pressure, wind, radiation, cloud and boundary-layer height on each region's local solar day. Earth Engine values were verified against an independent ARCO-ERA5 copy to within 0.001 K.

Provider ECMWF / Copernicus C3SAccess Google Earth EnginePeriod 2018–2025
Grid Definition

ERA5-Land (0.1°)

The land-surface replay of ERA5 at finer resolution, used to define the downscaling grid, as an input-resolution ablation, and as a plausibility reference. Coastal stations whose nearest 0.1° cell is ocean fall back to the nearest land cell.

Provider ECMWF / Copernicus C3SAccess Google Earth EnginePeriod 2018–2025
Ground Truth · Sandbox

GHCN-Daily, California Box

Daily maximum and minimum temperature from 490 stations between 32–42.5°N and 125–114°W, spanning coastal, desert, valley and mountain regimes. Morning observers are aligned to the afternoon they report.

Provider NOAA NCEIStations 490Role Training and evaluation
Ground Truth · Transfer

GHCNh and ISD, Horn of Africa

Hourly synoptic reports turned into daily extremes on a fixed sampling schedule, with per-station eligibility for maximum and minimum temperature. Named humanitarian sites include Kakuma and Kalobeyei, Dadaab, and Nairobi's informal settlements.

Provider NOAA NCEIStations entering 31Role Transfer evaluation
Covariates · Figures

MODIS and VIIRS Land Products

Vegetation, land cover, built-up and water indices as expert-only covariates, never inputs to the regime gate. Satellite land-surface temperature appears only in context figures, not as a model input.

Provider NASA LP DAACAccess Google Earth EngineRole Covariates and figures
